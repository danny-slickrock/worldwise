## What the pilot says about review volume

- 28 images for 5 countries, about 5.6 per country. At 196 countries that's roughly **1,000 images to review**
  (some countries have no World Heritage Site with an image, so somewhat fewer).
- About 3 of 28 needed a reviewer's judgment beyond "is this a good photo": the wrong kind of item
  (Kyoto Prefecture), a missing headline site (Machu Picchu), and an un-creditable file.
- Suggested before scaling: exclude administrative-area items, and consider a curated must-include per
  country (or rank by a fame signal other than sitelinks).
